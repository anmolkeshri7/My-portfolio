Hey everyone! I'm Anmol Keshri, a 20-year-old B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at Birla Institute of Technology (BIT), Mesra, Ranchi.

I'm passionate about coding, web development and technology. I enjoy learning new skills, experimenting with ideas, and developing projects that help me grow as a software developer.

My ambition goes beyond becoming a software engineer. I aspire to build successful businesses, become an entrepreneur, and create meaningful solutions to real-world problems.

Outside technology, cricket has been one of my biggest passions since childhood. I enjoy following matches, exploring player statistics and appreciating the strategy behind the game.

I'm also passionate about bikes, cars, road trips and the outdoors. Exploring the breathtaking Himalayas through trekking and adventure rides is one of my dreams.

My faith and personal values help keep me grounded. I believe in staying disciplined, remaining humble, and pursuing my goals while continuing to learn from every experience.

Through this website, I want to share my coding projects, learning experiences, entrepreneurial ideas and adventures. I believe that consistent effort, curiosity and the courage to try can take us a long way.
